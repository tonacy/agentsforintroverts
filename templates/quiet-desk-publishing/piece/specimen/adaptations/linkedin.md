The Agents for Introverts home page opens on a sea of feed fragments: a reply, an ask, a mention, a thread you were added to. It moves the way a feed does.

Then it slows down, and only as fast as you scroll. The motion is scrubbed by scroll, never timed, so it never runs ahead of the person reading. It slows, but it never stops.

If you prefer reduced motion, the page is simply at rest. Nothing on it depends on watching an animation.
