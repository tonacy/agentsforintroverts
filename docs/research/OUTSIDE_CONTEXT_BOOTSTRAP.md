# Outside-context bootstrap

Captured: 2026-08-20

## Readiness verdict

This is a useful seed corpus, not a live outside-world connection.

- Scope: 12 public source doors across agent-mediated identity, authorship,
  feeds and attention, standards, trust, and founder distribution.
- Observation method: bounded manual public-web review.
- Strongest coverage: durable concepts and current research around agents,
  authorship, and feed design.
- Weakest coverage: current person-level conversations outside Indie Hackers,
  non-English discourse, private communities, and actual replies or outcomes.
- Runtime proof still missing: the sources have not yet received persistent
  Quiet Hub `source.observed` receipts from a configured live bridge.

The corpus is sufficient to shape the lens and identify candidate questions. It
is not sufficient to claim that the daily agent knows what is happening across
the network, or to infer that a recurring topic is consensus.

## Durable reference doors

1. [Dittos: Mimetic, Reciprocal Agents in AI-Mediated Communication](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/dittos-mimetic-reciprocal-agents-in-ai-mediated-communication/)
   — the closest external parallel: personal proxy agents act while someone is
   absent and report back so human relationships can continue. It is a 2026
   position paper, not deployment proof.
2. [The AI Ghostwriter Effect](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03283) — empirical
   evidence that claiming authorship and feeling ownership can diverge, and that
   greater human influence increases ownership.
3. [U.S. Copyright Office: Copyrightability](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html)
   — a legal floor for human contribution: AI assistance can be part of a
   protectable work, while prompts alone do not establish sufficient authorship.
   Copyright is narrower than social authenticity.
4. [Into the Driver's Seat With Social Media Content Feeds](https://knightcolumbia.org/content/into-the-drivers-seat-with-social-media-content-feeds)
   — a legible model of feed selection and ranking, useful for designing a lens
   rather than treating every feed as a single undifferentiated mechanism.
5. [Algorithmic Choice with Custom Feeds](https://bsky.social/about/blog/7-27-2023-custom-feeds)
   — the constructive counterposition that algorithms can increase agency when
   people choose and replace their feeds; Bluesky also notes the power of defaults.
6. [POSSE](https://indieweb.org/POSSE) — a durable model for publishing a
   canonical original on a site one controls, then syndicating copies or links
   elsewhere. Destination behavior remains platform-dependent.

## Active research and standards doors

7. [What Do AI Agents Talk About?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07880) — a 2026
   study of Moltbook's first 23 days that reports extensive formulaic comments
   and declining coherence with thread depth. It is a warning that agent
   activity can resemble discourse without sustaining relationship.
8. [NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative](https://www.nist.gov/artificial-intelligence/ai-agent-standards-initiative)
   — an evolving 2026 program covering interoperability, security, identity,
   authentication, and authorization for agents acting on behalf of users. It
   is an initiative, not a completed standard.
9. [The AI penalty and disclosure paradox](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949882126000551)
   — a 2026 preregistered survey experiment reporting lower trust and
   authenticity ratings for AI-assisted or generated workplace and social text.
10. [Writing with AI boosts trust-building efficiency](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41492389/)
    — two preregistered trust-game experiments reporting minimal trust loss from
    predictive writing assistance, including with disclosure. The one-shot,
    transactional setting makes this a useful disagreement rather than a direct
    refutation of the social-writing result.

## Current conversation doors

11. [I can build anything. Distribution is the wall I keep hitting.](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-can-build-anything-distribution-is-the-wall-i-keep-hitting-694cd7f9bc)
    — a June 2026 founder conversation arguing that useful replies and repeated
    presence in the right community outperform broad posting, while separating
    engagement from demand.
12. [Build in public is a distribution strategy for founders selling to founders](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/build-in-public-is-a-distribution-strategy-for-founders-selling-to-founders-11f31744e4)
    — an August 2026 counterpoint: public founder discourse can be the wrong
    channel when buyers do not inhabit founder networks.

These two conversations are qualitative source doors, not market evidence or
proof of consensus.

## Tensions the lens must preserve

- Dittos proposes relational continuity; Moltbook research warns that proxy
  activity can become ritualized noise.
- Knight emphasizes feed legibility; Bluesky emphasizes feed plurality and user
  choice.
- The disclosure studies disagree across settings. Relationship depth and
  context may matter more than a single global disclosure rule.
- POSSE supplies a canonical human origin; each network still requires a native
  adaptation and its own proof of publication.
- Repeated useful participation may build recognition, but it cannot rescue a
  product that is speaking in the wrong room.

## Candidate insight, not yet a Place

Agents for Introverts should not be framed as a machine for posting more. The
outside research points toward a narrower product promise: preserve a legible
human origin, help a person notice a few real openings, and return enough of the
interaction that a relationship can develop. A Place should therefore require
an exact conversation door, confirmed fit, a useful contribution, and a return
path — not merely a relevant topic.

The two Indie Hackers discussions are plausible candidate Places because they
touch the founder's distribution blind spot and the difference between broad
presence and useful participation. They should not be surfaced as Places until
today's human capture is confirmed and the runtime source records are current.
