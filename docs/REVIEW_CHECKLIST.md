# What to review before activation

## 1. Human-thread judgment

- Does Threads feel like a small set of relationships worth developing rather than a quieter activity monitor?
- Does each thread explain the recurring common ground, why it fits this person's context, and why it matters now?
- Is disagreement preserved rather than flattened into synthetic consensus?
- Does the narrowing from broad discourse to shared intent to two or three people make sense from the cited evidence?
- Would each surfaced person recognize the claimed shared intent?
- Is Activity clearly secondary but still sufficient to audit every source claim?
- Can every claim be traced to the right source door without exposing unnecessary private text?

## 2. Living context

- Can the user see which context statements made a thread fit?
- Are explicit statements, observations, and inferences labeled distinctly?
- Does every observed or inferred statement retain resolvable evidence?
- Is it clear that context helps route attention but does not grant permission to speak?

## 3. Approval language

- Is it unmistakable that a proposal has not been approved or executed?
- Are provider acknowledgement, delivery, and read displayed as separate evidence?
- Does an edit invalidate the previous payload hash and revision?
- Is read-only mode the default you want for the first live source?
- Does a human handoff stop at one exact proposal rather than becoming automated outreach?

## 4. Agent judgment

- Do the seven role prompts sound like the same quiet practice while preserving distinct jobs?
- Does Common Ground do the cross-surface synthesis without turning the operational agents into broad social monitors?
- Which single source should become the first live, dedicated test scope?
- What should never be stored, even as an excerpt?
- Does the agent update a recurring thread instead of publishing duplicates from every network surface?
- Does it surface at most three people based on reciprocal intent, not merely topical similarity?

## 5. Native feel

- Is the density calm but still operational?
- Are keyboard navigation, search, source inspection, reduced motion, increased contrast, and empty/error states acceptable?
- Should Quiet Desk be the final product name?

## 6. Activation boundary

- Decide whether the next milestone is the read-only live hub adapter in the
  Mac app or the first provider source connector; the current Mac bundle remains
  synthetic even though the hub/bridge path is functional locally.
- Choose where to host Quiet Hub and the Grok-facing MCP bridge.
- Create separate Codex and Grok connection identities and secrets.
- Review retention/deletion policy before any personal source data enters the hub.
- Do not authorize an external executor in the first live test.
- Do not call context or common-ground projection live until the read-only hub
  adapter supplies the same records and exact evidence the Mac UI displays.

## 7. Authenticated sources and history

- On X, can the reviewer visibly prove that Following is selected before any
  post is read and again after navigation or reload?
- Does the run fail closed when X lands on For You, Explore, trends,
  notifications, promoted content, or another denied surface?
- On LinkedIn, are Sponsored, Promoted, Suggested, recommended, profile,
  relationship-graph, notification, message, invitation, and prospecting
  surfaces skipped?
- Are all social writes absent, including likes, bookmarks, follows, reactions,
  replies, messages, invitations, and posts?
- Are authenticated-feed and current-day Computer History cues kept ephemeral,
  omitted from `observe_source`, and deleted within 24 hours?
- Is an exact social item publicly revalidated and minimized before it becomes a
  source record, factual briefing claim, or Place?
- Does Computer History offer a question rather than infer intent, belief,
  emotion, priority, progress, completion, or a first-person account?
- Is only Tony's confirmed, retention-authorized excerpt admitted to the private
  daily capture?
- Is authenticated personal data absent from the current append-only Hub?
- Can the portable Quiet Desk archive be opened without Obsidian and recovered
  without Quiet Hub?
- Before Obsidian activation, are plugins, encryption, one sync authority,
  excluded caches, backup, and conflict recovery reviewed?
- Before automated history activation, can immutable local events rebuild all
  projections and SQLite after a correction, deletion, and edit conflict?

## 8. First-pilot trust contract

- Does every evaluation lead with a recommendation or an explicit "none worth
  recommending" result?
- Do the evaluated and rejected counts reconcile with the approach records?
- Can the reviewer drill into sources, method, results, rejection reasons,
  limitations, rationale, proof plan, and rollback?
- Was the evaluation bounded, read-only, reversible, and free of unauthorized
  spend, installs, provider mutations, or outreach?
- If a change set is PR-ready, are local tests and the frozen diff hash recorded
  without implying merge, deployment, or live impact?
- Does an intentional no-new-input check-in complete without inventing a capture,
  Place, task, or blocker?
- Can a planned social payload reach its approval surface without opening a
  discovery feed?
- Are account, target, payload, revision, links, media, and timing frozen before
  approval, with any edit invalidating approval?
- Is the publisher absent or still labeled unproven until identity, idempotency,
  execution, and public receipt are verified end to end?
- Before MCP activation, can the canonical proposal bind a revisioned human seed
  without requiring a fabricated public source for a source-free post?
- Did these pilots remain behind the Daily Conversation instead of creating a
  new top-level lane?
