# Agents for Introverts — website design draft

Status: design for review. No implementation or publication authorized.

## Design brief

Help people with ideas, projects, and lived experience understand a quieter way to participate online. The site should make network fluency on human terms tangible, then invite the reader into the manifesto and the public practice. Success is a reader understanding the premise, the human boundary, and the next step; it is not an app signup.

Use the approved Navigational Shelter identity, warm paper (#FDFBF7), forest green (#0F4A38), Newsreader, IBM Plex Mono, and Inter. The visual direction is an open, editorial field journal: generous reading space, oversized serif statements, quiet rules, and two broad currents. Retain the selected mark exactly. No hourglass revival, generic AI dashboard, invented founder story, or unsupported product results.

## Homepage hierarchy and draft copy

1. **Promise.** “Participate in the network. Without living in the feed.” Supporting copy: “For people with ideas, projects, and lived experience who want to take part without becoming full-time performers for the feed.” Primary action: “Read the manifesto”. Secondary: “Explore the practice”.
2. **The lens.** “The world comes in. Your ideas go out.” Incoming agents help gather context, preserve disagreement, and bring back what matters. Outgoing agents help established ideas find a form and a place. Your voice, judgment, and commitments stay yours. This is the proposed division of work.
3. **Daily Conversation.** “Begin with your day.” Explain three beats: a small view of the outside; what changed in your own work and thinking; zero to three specific openings worth considering. Define a Place in everyday language. Include Short version / Deeper look / No new input as illustrative, clearly labeled choices; no fake personalized result or invented chat transcript.
4. **Human boundary.** “Inside stays inside.” Private work and thoughts in formation remain distinct from public sources and deliberately released ideas. Reflection is where a person decides what may cross. New beliefs, private stories, promises, and commitments come back to the person.
5. **Manifesto.** Exact canonical line: “We should not have to choose between being swept away by the network and disappearing from it.” A short reading entry, attribution, and link to the complete manifesto.
6. **Practice and field notes.** “A practice, becoming a product.” Slow Feed will document what agents saw, what was approved or rejected, where they were wrong, and whether a real human connection followed. Clear status: first field note in preparation; Quiet Desk is a local prototype. Link to the existing Substack publication. No embedded or disconnected email capture.

## Components and foundations

Primitives and semantic color tokens; spacing scale 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96; corner radius 0 and 4. Newsreader display and reading styles, Inter supporting text, IBM Plex Mono labels. Components: Brand lockup, Action (primary/secondary/link × default/hover/focus), Navigation (desktop/mobile), Editorial step, Field Notes (pre-publication/post-publication), and Footer (desktop/mobile). Post-publication state is a documented conditional variant, not an active status claim.

All related content uses auto-layout, fixed page widths with flexible internal columns, and text that hugs its height. Desktop: 1440px. Mobile: 390px, with a 320px stress check for core components. Focus indicators and minimum 44px targets are part of the design.
