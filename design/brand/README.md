# Network-focus identity

The mark is hourglass-shaped, but it is not a timepiece. Two graph fields
coalesce around one point of human focus. The left field represents the
outside network coming in; the right field represents lived context and
expression going out.

## Rules

- Preserve the two-way graph topology and the single central focus point.
- Do not add sand, clocks, countdowns, people, faces, or gendered pairings.
- Use the detailed network field for editorial and social artwork.
- Use the seven-node optical reduction for navigation and app icons.
- Keep the palette to warm paper (`#FDFBF7`) and forest green (`#0F4A38`)
  when the mark is shown alone.

- `network-focus-favicon-source.png`: optically corrected small-icon source.

The source artwork was generated with built-in ImageGen. The social-card
prompt asked for two opposing graph fields converging at a central green focus
point on warm editorial paper, with Newsreader-like typography and no literal
hourglass. The small-icon prompt retained exactly three nodes per side, enlarged
the center, removed fine gaps, and prohibited timepiece, human, insect, and
bow-tie cues.
