## LinkedIn

Every day in Quiet Desk starts with a capture: a short file I write myself, marked `author: human` at the top.

The runner has four rules for it. It quotes it. It hashes it. It refuses to run without it. And it never edits it. No typo fixes, no smoothing, no summary standing in for what I said.

The decision here is less about the agent and more about having one fixed point. Whatever the runner produces that day can be read next to the words it started from, and those words haven't moved.
